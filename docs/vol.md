# VOL (Volume) Indicator

## Overview

The Volume (VOL) indicator is a basic but essential tool in technical analysis. It simply represents the trading volume for each period and is often used as a foundation for more complex volume-based indicators. Volume reflects the number of shares, contracts, or units traded during a specific period and is a key measure of market activity and liquidity.

## Calculation

The VOL indicator is calculated as follows:

- For each period, the volume value is taken directly from the data series.

## Usage

- **Trend Confirmation:** High volume during price moves can confirm the strength of a trend.
- **Reversal Signals:** Spikes in volume may signal potential reversals or the start of new trends.
- **Support/Resistance:** Volume can help identify significant support or resistance levels.
- **Foundation for Other Indicators:** VOL is used in the calculation of many other indicators, such as OBV, MFI, and VWAP.
